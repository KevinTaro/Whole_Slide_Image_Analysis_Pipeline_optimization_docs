## Arm model (current, at the real slide's composition)

`wall ≈ max(MAIN, BG) + outside`. At `match24` (55.9% background, `workers=1`): **MAIN 187.4 s, BG
88.0 s, outside 7.5 s → BG/MAIN = 0.470 → MAIN must shed 53.0% before the BG arm (`detect_all_dots`,
PNG encode) becomes critical.** This is *wider* slack than every tissue-dense crop measured before
round 7 implied (as little as 15.9%–28%) — more tissue tiles load MAIN faster than they load BG, the
opposite of what a "mostly background" slide would predict. Full detail:
[`25-gpu-encode-decode-loop-acceleration-implementation.md`](../25-gpu-encode-decode-loop-acceleration-implementation.md) §2.3–§2.4.

## Memory (bounded — claim holds; host requirements below are the real full-slide numbers)

Crop-scale VRAM peak (single-process): **2787 MB**, flat regardless of tile count. Under
multiprocessing, VRAM per process grows **superlinearly** with worker count (2787 → 3117 → 4118 →
5167 MB at N=1–4 on crops) — this, not model-weight size, is what caps the safe worker count. Full
detail: [21-cross-tile-multiprocessing-implementation.md](../21-cross-tile-multiprocessing-implementation.md)
§4.4, [25-...](../25-gpu-encode-decode-loop-acceleration-implementation.md) §8.3.

**Real full-slide numbers, round 8** (`workers=4` VRAM, driven by the stitch holding 27,565 lazy
pyvips images, 12,027 open fds mid-stitch): peak RSS **61.13 GB (`workers=1`) / 61.67 GB
(`workers=4`)**; peak GPU **2,739 MB (`workers=1`) / 30,439 MB (`workers=4`, 93.3% of the
32,607 MB card, ~2.2 GB headroom)**. Host requirements this implies: **~64 GB RAM, ~32 GB VRAM for
`workers=4`, ~350 GB disk per slide, `RLIMIT_NOFILE` ≥ ~28,000**. Full detail:
[27-...](../27-remaining-work-implementation.md) §6.4, §6.6. **Round 10/11 (`workers=1`, after
`d6592c3` removed 275 GB/slide of per-tile intermediate writes): peak RSS 60.04–60.17 GB** — down
slightly but the same order of magnitude; `workers=4` RSS/VRAM have not been re-measured since
round 8. Round 11's `expandable_segments:True` sweep separately found `workers=4` peak GPU
framebuffer can reach **92.2% of the card (30.1 GB)** with that knob on — see the allocator row
above; this is a ceiling from a different experiment, not a new steady-state number.

**All of the above (rounds 8–11) was measured on the wrong canvas.** Doc 44 found the full-slide
runs from round 8 through round 14 fed an unregistered, incorrectly-sized input; the correct,
registered canvas is 35,700 tiles / 20.94 GP / 65.92% background, not 27,565 / 55.8%. **Round 15
re-measured RSS on the corrected canvas: peak RSS 13.66 GB (`workers=1`) / 13.24 GB (`workers=4`)**
— down 77.6% from round 8's figure, both from the round-13 stitch-backend change (band streaming
replaced a lazy whole-slide join) and from the round-8 baseline itself having been measured on the
wrong, since-superseded input. VRAM has not been re-measured on the corrected canvas; treat the
round-8/11 GPU figures above as the best available estimate. See
[`44`](../44-conform-intersection-shift-investigation.md),
[`46`](../46-round-15-eta-estimation-implementation.md) §3.7, §5.4.
